SMS Opt-In Policy

Give A Meal (Numbers Office LLC) · Last updated May 19, 2026

Service description

Give A Meal operates a conversational, customer-initiated text messaging service (the “SMS Service”). Recipients text our published phone number to find and reserve nearby donated meals and receive a 6-digit redemption code that they show at the participating restaurant to pick up their meal.

Opt-in method

This is a conversational, customer-initiated messaging use case. We do not send unsolicited or promotional messages. Recipients opt in by initiating contact with us first — they text our number to find and reserve a nearby meal.

Our phone number is made available through the following channels:

  • Published on our website at https://www.give-a-meal.org
  • Shared directly with customers who contact us through other channels (phone, email, in person)

When a customer texts us first, that inbound message constitutes their consent to receive a reply. We only respond to customers who have messaged us; we do not initiate outbound campaigns, send marketing content, or message recipients who have not contacted us first.

Message frequency

Low and variable. Messages are sent only in direct response to a customer’s inquiry, typically a handful of messages per conversation.

Message content

Replies are transactional and tied to the customer’s inquiry. Typical content includes confirmation of an available meal, the name and address of the participating restaurant, and the 6-digit redemption code the customer presents at pickup.

Standard disclaimers

  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Customers can reply STOP to any message at any time to opt out. After we receive a STOP request, we will no longer send SMS messages to that number.
  • Because we only reply to inbound messages, customers will not receive any messages unless they have first contacted us.
  • Customers can also request removal of their phone number and conversation history at any time by emailing max@give-a-meal.org.
No marketing, no outbound campaigns. We do not run promotional or marketing SMS campaigns. We never initiate contact with users who have not first texted us. Every outbound message is a direct reply to an inbound user message.
